# Production workflow

Read this before making a motion video, recording production assets or renders, or recording feedback or approval.

## Actions

Select by the requested action, not the subject noun. Every action belongs to `production:production`.

| Action | Additional required instructions |
| --- | --- |
| Make a new motion video, reel, music video, greeting, explainer, or animated piece from a blank or partial idea | `motion-design`; read `essential:skills/discover/SKILL.md` for the interview and board |
| Register footage, audio, graphics, fonts, LUTs, templates, or generated media; record a render/export; declare provenance, rights, or delivery | `track`; read `essential:references/state.md`, Essential's shared domain README templates, and `production:skills/production/templates/asset-manifest.md` |
| Review a cut or render; record feedback, approval, or approval validity after a change | `review`; read `essential:references/state.md`, `essential:references/approvals.md`, and `essential:references/reviews.md` |

`track` and `review` write versioned manifests and review records. They never edit media bytes, invent provenance or rights facts, or carry approval across a revision without explicit preserved scope. `motion-design` records every asset and render it produces through `track`.

When establishing a production work stream, declare a `media-project` or `asset-store` anchor per `essential:references/anchors.md`. Essential owns state, decisions, journal, and handover through its state lifecycle contract.

## Standards

Production owns no standards. Follow the selected action and the named Essential lifecycle contracts; do not import standards from an undeclared plugin.
