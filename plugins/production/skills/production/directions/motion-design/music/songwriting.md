# Songwriting

Read from `directions/motion-design/music.md` when writing lyrics, a hook, or the musical material a composition plan carries. Pick the song's structure from the [song recipes](directions/motion-design/music/song-recipes.md) (M1–M8) first, then write it with the craft below.

## Before writing

- Name the emotional route in verbs (hesitate, try, fail, understand, act), not adjectives such as "epic".
- Choose the music's point of view (share the character's confidence, reveal what they miss, or observe) and mark where it must land or step back. Picture shows evidence, words give perspective, music shapes expectation.

## Hook, melody, and prosody

- Start from one truthful thought and a small memorable gesture; try several melodies on the same words before expanding.
- Prosody: meaning, word stress, musical emphasis, rhyme, and form serve one intent. Make the key word easy to hear, not merely the highest note; keep connecting words light; leave breath; give held notes open vowels.
- One sonic signature (question motif, answering instrument, rhythmic gesture), not every instrument competing.
- Form by function: establish, complicate, arrive, reconsider, return. Verses add information, the refrain gains meaning from them, a bridge changes perspective.
- A prompt naming notes does not make a music model a notation engine; when exact notes must survive, keep an editable instrumental, MIDI, or permitted recording.

## Write lyrics in seven passes

1. One speaker: a private thought and a brand promise are different voices.
2. The truthful prose version; a sung exaggeration is still a claim.
3. Observable task, object, or place instead of abstract praise.
4. Speak it, mark the natural stresses, and put the musical emphasis there unless an exception usefully changes the meaning.
5. A melodic sentence: modest range, repetition, a contrasting answer, one peak, then ornament.
6. Rhyme last; near, internal, or no rhyme beats bending the message.
7. Sing it against the picture at pace; rewrite lines that cannot be delivered.

Bilingual group-chant lyrics worked for a birthday piece; run pass 4 in each language. Keep lyrics apart from style instructions, request instrumental gaps explicitly, and confirm each gap, and the absence of extra words or a spoken intro, on the measured take ([music](directions/motion-design/music.md) step 5).

## Adjust and check

Audition adjustments, not emotional codes (minor is not automatically sad): change a contour's ending at the turn, delay resolution, change rhythmic spacing before tempo, pass an idea between instruments, thin layers around the key lyric.

Before generating: the lyric adds meaning rather than naming every visible noun; no twist is revealed early; the key word and key image are both perceivable; the shape fits the form (a visualiser may need no climax); melody, lyric, and signature are original, and any existing music is licensed; the last breath and decay sit inside the runtime.
