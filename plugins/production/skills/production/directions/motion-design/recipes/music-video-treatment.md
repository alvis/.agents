# Music video treatment

Read from `directions/motion-design/recipes/music-video.md` once a V recipe is chosen, before the storyboard is locked.

Turn a chosen V recipe into a treatment and animatic. The recipes and shelf rules live in [music video recipes](directions/motion-design/recipes/music-video.md).

## Treatment to animatic

- **Governing relationship**: complete "When the music does X, the image does Y, because Z"; the reason matters more than the effect.
- **Map the take**: mark phrases, vocal entries, instrumental answers, rests, boundaries, and returns. Beat detection and transcripts are aids to correct against the performance; a supplied song's audio map is the source ([music](directions/motion-design/music.md)).
- **Three scales of sync**: an accent meets a gesture, a phrase organises an action, a section changes the visual system; each accent anticipates, arrives, or follows. Keep sparse stretches so the refrain has contrast.
- **Visual grammar**: identity anchors (proportions, location, materials, palette, light direction, camera language, transitions) go in the storyboard style sheet. At the final refrain change one meaningful property, such as who owns the motif or whether an empty space is filled; more particles is not development.
- **Animatic**: placeholders timed to the locked take, signature recurring shot first. Check pacing, contrast, lyric readability, and ending; fix a weak treatment here, not with polish.
- **Shot spec**: start composition, action, end composition, duration, continuity anchors, handles, and the phrase served. Render Blender or generated shots with handles so a cut can move without re-rendering.
- **Lyrics and lip-sync**: lyric type can follow phrases with no singing face. Lock and [align](directions/motion-design/music/alignment.md) the vocal before mouth animation; a late lyric fix reopens those shots. Never stack dense text, a complex lyric, and a key action at once.
- **Review**: watch with sound, then silent for clutter; no rapid flashing as default excitement; cut any excerpt at a phrase arrival.
