# Story devices

The catalogue the `motion-design` action draws story concepts from. A device is the narrative shape that carries the facts; the same facts told through different devices make different videos.

| Device | Shape | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toast round | Friends raise glasses one by one, each toast opens a flashback | Birthdays, farewells, weddings |
| Surprise party | Build-up to a reveal the subject does not see coming | Birthdays, launches |
| Origin story | Hero's beginnings, the moment powers appeared, the first battle | Founders, tributes, careers |
| Level-up game | Life as video game levels with bosses, power-ups and a final level | Developers, gamers, milestones |
| Movie poster parade | Each chapter is a fake film poster or title card | Tributes with many eras |
| Mockumentary | Deadpan interviews about the subject's legend | Comedy, roasts, team videos |
| Nature documentary | A narrator observes the subject in the wild | Comedy, habits, quirks |
| Heist | A crew plans and pulls off a caper that turns out to be the surprise | Surprises, team efforts |
| Recipe | Ingredients that make the person or product, then the dish served | Tributes, brand values |
| Unboxing | Opening a box reveals the person's traits or a product's features | Products, playful tributes |
| Museum tour | A guide walks through exhibits of a life or company | Retrospectives, anniversaries |
| Map journey | A dotted line travels between places, each stop a scene | Travel, moves, homecomings |
| Time machine | Dials spin to years, each landing on a moment | Birthdays, anniversaries |
| Trading cards | Stats, rarity and special moves for each person or feature | Teams, sports, gamers |
| Book or comic chapters | Pages turn through chapters with titles | Life stories, children |
| Court trial | Evidence presented that the subject is guilty of being awesome | Comedy, roasts |
| Weather forecast | A presenter forecasts the subject's year ahead | Birthdays, new-year wishes |
| News broadcast | Breaking-news anchors report the event | Announcements, milestones |
| Construction build | A thing is assembled piece by piece, each piece a story | Makers, launches, teams |
| Day in the life | One day from sunrise to night, each hour a trait | Personal portraits, brands |
| Letters across time | Messages from past and future selves | Emotional milestones |
| Award ceremony | Categories announced, the subject wins every one | Tributes, team thanks |
| Music video | The subject stars in a song about themselves | Celebrations, anthems |
| Choose-your-path | The video branches at a decision, both paths shown | Products, playful stories |
