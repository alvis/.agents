# Visual styles

The catalogue the `motion-design` action suggests style directions from. Each style names the look, the motion grammar that keeps it consistent, and what it suits. **3D** marks styles that need Three.js or Blender beyond flat web animation.

| Style | Look | Motion grammar | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Halftone comic | Ben-Day dots, ink outlines, speech bubbles, burst captions | Panel cuts, slam-ins with overshoot, impact lines | Celebrations, heroes, playful energy |
| Risograph print | Two or three spot inks, misregistered layers, grain | Layers slide out of register then lock, paper jitter | Indie, music, zines, warm nostalgia |
| Flat vector | Solid shapes, no outlines, bold palette | Smooth eases, shape morphs, masked reveals | Explainers, products, clean brand work |
| Cut-out paper collage | Torn paper, photo scraps, tape, visible shadows | Stop-motion stepping at 12 fps, pieces flutter in | Personal stories, scrapbooks, family |
| Claymation look | Rounded forms, fingerprint texture, soft studio light (**3D**) | Squash and stretch, stepped frames, slight boil | Warm comedy, kids, characters |
| Low-poly 3D | Faceted geometry, flat-shaded light (**3D**) | Slow orbit cameras, pieces assembling | Places, worlds, building things |
| Isometric diorama | Tiny 2.5D world on a tilted grid | Camera glides between rooms, objects pop up | Houses, offices, journeys, systems |
| Pixel art | 8- or 16-bit sprites, limited palette, chiptune pairing | Stepped motion, sprite cycles, level transitions | Developers, gamers, retro humour |
| Blueprint | White line work on blue paper, dimensions, grid | Lines draw themselves, plans extrude | Builders, engineers, makers |
| Chalkboard or whiteboard | Hand-drawn strokes on dark or white board | Write-on lines, erase wipes | Teaching, recaps, origin stories |
| Watercolour storybook | Soft washes, paper bleed, handwritten captions | Page turns, washes bleeding in | Tender messages, children, memories |
| Ink brush (sumi-e) | Black brush strokes, negative space, red seal | Strokes paint on, slow holds | Calm, reflective, elegant pieces |
| Kinetic typography | Type is the picture | Words punch, stretch and stack on the beat | Lyrics, manifestos, announcements |
| Swiss modernist | Grid, sans-serif, primary colours, asymmetry | Hard cuts, sliding blocks on the grid | Brands, conferences, data |
| Memphis 80s | Squiggles, confetti shapes, clashing pastels | Bouncy pops, rotations, pattern scrolls | Parties, retro fun |
| Synthwave | Neon grids, sunsets, chrome type | Slow forward dolly, scanlines, glow pulses | Music, nostalgia, nightlife |
| Y2K glossy | Chrome blobs, bubble type, iridescent gradients | Floaty bobs, lens flares, glossy sweeps | Fashion, pop, teen energy |
| Vintage cartoon (1930s rubber hose) | Pie-cut eyes, sepia, film grain | Rubber-hose bounce on every beat, iris wipes | Absurd comedy, toasts, tributes |
| Anime | Cel shading, speed lines, dramatic skies | Smear frames, held poses, impact zooms | Epic or emotional character beats |
| Retro travel poster | Flat lithograph colour, bold sans, landscapes | Parallax layers, slow pans | Places, homecomings, travel |
| Polaroid and photo montage | Real photos in frames, handwriting, pins | Photos drop and stack, Ken Burns drift | Life stories, recaps, tributes |
| Glassmorphism 3D | Frosted glass shapes, soft light, depth (**3D**) | Slow rotations, refraction sweeps | Tech brands, product launches |
| Papercraft 3D | Folded paper in real light (**3D**) | Pop-up book unfolds, folds hinge open | Storybook worlds, invitations |
| Embroidery and textile | Stitches, fabric texture, buttons | Thread stitches in, fabric ripples | Handmade, family, craft |
| Mid-century modern | Atomic shapes, muted teal and mustard, film grain | Limited animation, sliding cut-outs | Retro charm, design lovers |
| Graffiti and street | Spray paint, tags, brick walls | Spray-on reveals, fast handheld shake | Music, youth, bold statements |
