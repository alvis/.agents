# Platforms

How a viewing platform shapes the brief. Platform limits change; check the platform's current upload specification before rendering a final.

| Platform | Aspect and canvas | Typical length | Sound | Layout consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website hero or background | 16:9 (1920×1080), or wider for a banner | 6–20 s seamless loop | Autoplays muted | The message must read without sound; music is optional and off by default. Keep the file small for page weight. |
| Website section, landing page, product page | 16:9 | 30–120 s | Plays on click | Sound can carry the piece; open with a strong first frame because it doubles as the poster. |
| Social reel (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) | 9:16 (1080×1920) | 15–60 s | Mostly on, often muted on first view | Hook in the first second; burn in the catchphrases; keep text and faces out of the top and bottom bands where platform buttons and captions sit. |
| Social feed post (Instagram, LinkedIn, X) | 4:5 (1080×1350) or 1:1 | 10–45 s | Autoplays muted | Burned-in text carries the message; the first frame must work as a still. |
| YouTube or presentation | 16:9 | Any | On | Room for narration and slower pacing; design a thumbnail frame. |
| Personal message (birthday, invitation, announcement) | 9:16 or 1:1 | 15–45 s | On | Personal names and photos lead; keep the file within the messenger's attachment limit. |
| Screen or event display | Match the screen, often 16:9 or portrait signage | Loop | Usually silent | No reliance on sound; type sized for viewing distance. |
