# Character styles

The catalogue the `motion-design` action suggests character and mascot styles from when a real person, pet, or brand figure appears on screen. Each style names how much likeness it keeps and how much it costs to animate.

| Style | Look | Likeness | Animation cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Comic caricature | Ink outlines, exaggerated signature features | High | Medium |
| Chibi | Big head, tiny body, huge eyes | Medium | Low |
| Bobblehead | Real-proportioned head on a small springy body | High | Low |
| Funko-style vinyl figure | Square head, dot eyes, glossy toy finish | Low; carried by hair, beard, clothes | Low |
| Lego-style minifigure | Blocky parts, printed face, claw hands | Low; carried by outfit and props | Low |
| Flat geometric mascot | Circles and rounded rectangles, no outlines | Low | Very low |
| Rubber-hose cartoon | 1930s noodle limbs, white gloves, pie eyes | Medium | Medium |
| Pixel sprite | 32 to 64 px sprite with a walk cycle | Low; carried by colour and silhouette | Low |
| Claymation figure | Soft clay forms, visible fingerprints (**3D**) | Medium | High |
| 3D toy or Pixar-style render | Smooth stylised 3D with soft light (**3D**) | Medium | High |
| Anime character | Cel-shaded, expressive eyes, hair as a shape | Medium | Medium |
| Photo cut-out head | The real face photo on a drawn or collage body | Exact | Low |
| Paper puppet | Jointed cut-out limbs on split pins | Medium | Low |
| Line-art portrait | Single continuous line drawing | High | Low |
| Silhouette | Solid shape against light | Low; carried by outline | Very low |
| Animal alter ego | The person as an animal chosen for their traits | Symbolic | Medium |
| Superhero version | The person in a costume themed on their job or passion | Medium | Medium |
| Emoji or sticker set | Flat sticker faces with a white die-cut border | Low | Very low |
